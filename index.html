Becoming a Denton firefighter

Does Denton have its own fire academy? Will you train me from zero?

No. Denton does not run a recruit academy that trains people from scratch.

New hires arrive already certified. Earn your certifications at any Texas Commission on Fire Protection (TCFP) approved academy and a state-approved EMT program, then test with us. Nearby options include North Central Texas College, Tarrant County College, and Weatherford College. Denton ISD students can use the LaGrone Fire and EMT academies.

The one exception is paramedics. See the paramedic question below.

Do you have a paid "recruit while you train" program?

Only for paramedics.

We do not hire people without certifications and put them through a fire academy. The one exception is a Texas-certified Paramedic: if you are hired as a Paramedic without a fire certification, the department pays for your fire academy after hire. Everyone else needs TCFP firefighter and Texas EMT-Basic before the conditional offer.

What are the minimum requirements?
  • 18 or older, and you must not have reached your 36th birthday on the exam date (Texas Local Government Code 143.023)
  • High school diploma or GED
  • TCFP Basic Structural Fire Protection certification plus a Texas EMT-Basic, or a Texas Paramedic certification, by the date of hire. Finishing fire school and EMT school is not the same as holding the certifications: you also have to pass the state exams and be issued the TCFP certification and the Texas DSHS EMT certification.
  • Valid driver's license, with a Class B Texas license (or approved equivalent) within one year of hire
  • Able to read, write, and speak English
  • No felony convictions
  • Pass a background check, polygraph, drug screen, Physical Ability Test, medical exam, and psychological evaluation
  • Normal color vision, with vision in both eyes correctable to 20/20
Is there a maximum age? Can veterans get a waiver?

Yes, and no waiver is available.

Denton is a Texas civil service city. State law (Local Government Code 143.023) does not allow anyone who is 36 or older to take the entrance exam or be certified for an entry-level firefighter position. The law has no exception for military service.

Do you hire EMTs or paramedics only, without fire certification?

Every Denton position is dual role: firefighter and EMT both. We do not have single-role EMT or medic jobs.

The paramedic exception: a Texas-certified Paramedic can be hired without a fire certification. The department pays for the fire academy after hire. Paramedics also earn certification pay.

I'm already a career firefighter. Do you take lateral transfers?

Not at this time. Denton Fire does not currently have a lateral entry program.

Every new firefighter, including experienced firefighters from other departments and the military, enters through the Civil Service entrance exam and starts as a beginning firefighter. If you already hold both TCFP firefighter and Texas Paramedic certifications, your probationary period is 12 months instead of up to 18.

Do I need to be a paramedic?

No. TCFP firefighter plus Texas EMT-Basic meets the requirement. Paramedics earn $100 a month in certification pay on top of base salary, plus $2 an hour when assigned to ride a medic unit.

What does it pay?

Starting salary is $86,028.80 a year, with step raises up to a base of $108,014.40. Firefighters can also earn incentive pay for education, TCFP certifications, and EMS certification. Operations personnel work 24 hours on and 48 hours off on A, B, or C shift.

Do you offer internships?

No. The best hands-on options are Explorer Post 1291 (ages 14 to 20) and, once you are an applicant or a fire or EMS student, a ride-along.

Can I volunteer with the fire department?

Denton Fire is a career department and does not use volunteer firefighters. Teens 14 to 20 can join Explorer Post 1291, and adults can watch for the Citizens Fire Academy.

I'm in paramedic school. Do you hire EMTs to work the ambulance in the meantime?

No. Our ambulances are staffed by firefighters, so we do not hire single-role EMTs. Private ambulance services, hospital emergency departments, and event medical companies in the area often hire EMT-Basics and can work around a school schedule. Once you finish, you can test with us as a Paramedic.

Certifications

Do I need to be certified to register or take the exam?

No. You can register and test while you are still in school.

Your certifications must be in hand by the date of hire. Conditional offers usually go out in early spring, so plan to finish before then.

I registered but have no certifications yet. What happens if I pass?

You can go through every step of the process, including the Physical Ability Test, background, and both interview panels.

The only thing you cannot receive until you are certified is a conditional job offer. To be eligible for hire you need a TCFP firefighter certification plus a Texas EMT-Basic, or a Texas Paramedic.

The eligibility list lasts a full year, and some years we hire in the spring and again in the fall, so passing now gives you time to finish certifications while you are on the list. It is a heavy lift, but candidates have completed both fire and EMT in under three months. Contact the recruiter and we will connect you with our Training Division to plan the fastest path.

Which EMT or fire program do you recommend? Do online or hybrid programs count?

We do not endorse a specific school. Any state-approved EMT-Basic program that leads to NREMT and Texas DSHS certification counts the same, whether traditional or hybrid. Fire certification must come from a TCFP-approved academy. Many local colleges offer evening and part-time tracks.

I'm certified in another state or by the military. How do I transfer to Texas?

Fire: TCFP accepts IFSAC certificates. Upload your Firefighter I, Firefighter II, Hazmat Awareness, and Hazmat Operations seals to your TCFP FIDO account, add your EMT documentation, and complete the fingerprint background check. Training without IFSAC seals can go through a TCFP records review. Details: TCFP, Transferring to Texas. TCFP phone: 512-936-3838.

EMS: National Registry (NREMT) certification is the path to a Texas EMT or Paramedic certification through DSHS. Details: DSHS EMS reciprocity.

Will I have to go through a fire academy again if I'm already certified?

No. If you hold a TCFP firefighter certification, you will not repeat fire school. New hires do complete Denton's own onboarding and training.

Can you take my certification exams or proctor TCFP tests?

The department is not a TCFP testing site. Contact your training provider or TCFP for testing locations and dates.

The Civil Service exam

When and where is the next exam?

Saturday, January 9, 2027, at 10:00 a.m.
University Gateway Center, 801 N Texas Blvd, Denton, TX 76201

No one is admitted after 10:00 a.m. Bring a valid photo ID. Phones, bags, watches, food, and drinks are not allowed in the testing room.

How do I register?

Register for the exam here by December 28, 2026. Only candidates who register before the deadline will be permitted to take the exam. The exam is free.

Not ready to test this year? Join our recruit list to learn more and hear when the next registration opens.

How do I study? Which test is it (CEB, FACT, NFST)?

The exam is administered by Fire and Police Selection, Inc. (FPSI). Their Candidate Orientation Guide is the study guide we recommend. A score of 70 or higher places you on the commission list.

Can I earn extra points? Do veterans get points?

Yes. Points are added to a passing score:

  • +5 military service: DD-214 showing at least 180 days of active duty and an honorable discharge
  • +1 Denton resident: proof of residency
  • +1 LaGrone Fire/EMT graduate: completion certificate

Submit your documents through the Extra Points page before the date of the exam.

When will the job posting open?

There is no separate job posting. Exam registration is the application.

Registration opens once a year in the fall, and the exam is in January. Everyone hired in the following year comes from that exam's list. If you missed this year's registration, join our recruit list so you hear when the next one opens.

How many people are you hiring?

It depends on vacancies, so we do not set a number in advance. The eligibility list stays valid for one year from the test date, and some years we hire off it in both the spring and the fall. If you hear we are "not hiring," that usually means the current list is in use. The way in is to register and test when the next exam opens.

I need a testing accommodation.

Send your request by the registration deadline to the Civil Service Director at 601 E. Hickory Street, Suite A, Denton, TX 76205, or civilservice@cityofdenton.com. Include a current letter from a licensed professional on letterhead, a description of the functional limitation as it applies to a multiple-choice exam, and the accommodation you need.

Hiring steps after the exam

  1. Civil Service exam in January.
  2. Personal History Statement for the top-ranked candidates.
  3. Background investigation and polygraph. The polygraph is done remotely through a mobile app.
  4. Physical Ability Test in the spring, for candidates who pass background.
  5. Two interview panels on the same day.
  6. Conditional offer, contingent on medical, psychological, and certifications.
  7. Appointment letter and start date.
What is on the Physical Ability Test?

Candidates wear a 50 lb vest throughout. Event 1 is a stair climb with two extra 12.5 lb shoulder weights (4:00 limit). After a short rest, Events 2 through 6 run back to back on a second clock:

  1. Ladder raise and extension
  2. Equipment carry (two saws, 75 ft and back)
  3. Charged hose drag and pull
  4. Sledge swing
  5. Rescue drag (about 165 lb mannequin)

It is pass or fail. Read the PAT Prep guide for event-by-event training.

I have a medical condition. Will it disqualify me?

Most conditions are evaluated individually. The pre-employment medical exam follows NFPA 1582 and happens only after a conditional offer, as federal law requires. We cannot pre-screen anyone medically. If you are unsure, ask your doctor or an occupational medicine physician to review your condition against NFPA 1582 before you invest in training.

Can any part of the process be done remotely?

The polygraph is remote. The written exam, Physical Ability Test, and interviews are in person in Denton.

Ride-alongs

Who can do a ride-along?

Under department policy, ride-alongs are open to:

  • Students currently enrolled in a participating Fire or EMS program, or another Denton Fire partner agency
  • City of Denton employees and their family members, 16 and older
  • Members of Explorer Post 1291
  • Citizens Fire Academy participants
  • Denton Fire applicants who have submitted a Personal History Statement and passed background

Requests are reviewed and scheduled by the Emergency Services/Operations Assistant Chief, generally at least a week out. Riders must be 18 or older (16 and 17 with a guardian-signed release), complete HIPAA training, sign an observer agreement and a liability release, and be in good health that day. Wear a collared shirt, slacks, and closed shoes. No shorts, t-shirts, sandals, torn jeans, or hats in the apparatus. Weapons are not allowed. Riders observe only and do not handle equipment.

Can a ride-along count toward my clinical, nursing, or community service hours?

No.

Ride-alongs are observation only. Riders do not provide patient care, so they cannot satisfy EMT, paramedic, or nursing clinical requirements or community service hours. Your school's clinical coordinator can help you find an approved clinical site. If a school wants to explore an affiliation for future students, the school should contact the recruiter. Affiliations are set up between the department and a school's program, not for one student at a time.

I'm not eligible. Is there another way to learn about the job?

Yes. Stop by any station and say hello when the crew is in quarters, or request a station tour through the Public Event Request Form.

Explorer Post 1291 (ages 14 to 20)

How do I join?

Apply online at joinexploring.org and select Post 1291. Our Post Advisor will follow up with the meeting schedule. Parents can complete the application with their teen.

Do we have to live in Denton? Can my child join mid-year?

The program is built for Denton County youth. If you live outside the county, email the recruiter and we will check with the Post Advisor. New Explorers are welcome any time of year, and all we ask is regular attendance. Homeschool students apply the same way as everyone else.

I missed a meeting. What now?

Nothing is lost. Reply to your Post 1291 email or contact the recruiter, and we will connect you with the Post Advisor for the next date.

Events, visits, and presentations

How do I request a fire truck, station tour, safety talk, career day visit, or a program for my community group?

Use the Public Event Request Form at least one month before the date. Requests are fulfilled as submitted unless we contact you about a conflict or denial, so no reply means you are on the schedule. Community groups of any kind, from neighborhood associations to retiree and senior groups, use the same form.

Already invited us by email or sent your own participation form? Please still submit the Public Event Request Form. It is how a crew gets scheduled for your date.

On-duty crews handle most visits. They may be called away to an emergency, and they can usually stay about 30 minutes.

I'm a reporter or student working on a story. Who do I contact?

Media requests go to the department's Public Information Officer through the Fire Administration line, 940-349-8840.

Can you send me a Denton Fire patch?

No. We don't have a patch program, so we're not able to mail patches. Thanks for thinking of Denton.

I need an incident report or EMS record.

Use the City's Incident Reports, EMS Records, and Open Records Requests link on the Fire Department page.
